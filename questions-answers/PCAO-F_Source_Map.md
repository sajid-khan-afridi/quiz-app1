# PCAO-F Practice Quiz — Source and Coverage Map

**CCAO-F-aligned revision: 11 September 2026. Contains answers and objectives; open after the quiz.**

## Scope, authority, and method

The [Official CCAO-F guide, v1.0 (July 2026)](sandbox:/workspace/scratch/c90fcd5089a7/upload/Claude+Certified+Associate+–+Foundations+Exam+Guide(5).pdf) was supplied in this conversation and read in full for this revision. Its scope and audience are on pp. 1–3, format on p. 3, weights on p. 4, detailed objectives on pp. 4–5, and preparation guidance on pp. 6–7. Its blueprint is the authority for this CCAO-F-aligned edition. The [PCAO-F certification page](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications/pcao-f) supplies the study-source links and distinguishes PCAO-F's broader scope from CCAO-F.

This version implements the supplied CCAO-F revision brief and its implementation plan: 50 original/adapted questions plus 10 exact imports from Matthew Purcell's practice set. The imported stems, option text, and option order were preserved; only local numbering, line wrapping, and Markdown presentation changed. Their source keys were checked against the PDF. Explanations were written or adapted locally, with the scenario's qualifications preserved. Neither source represents live exam items.

Each item has one primary domain and one primary objective. Direct secondary objectives record additional decisions required by the item and do not increase domain totals. M-prefixed identifiers identify Matthew's items; objective numbers such as 2.2 are local labels for the official guide's bullet order and are not published exam codes. PDF page numbers refer to the viewer's one-based page count.

The ten course references S01–S10 are carried forward from the supplied baseline source map and answer key, whose prior review was dated 10 September 2026. During this revision, the official guide and Matthew PDF were read directly, and relevant sections of S03, S05, S06, and S09 were retrieved to check updated wording. S08 could not be retrieved through the live lookup during this pass; its baseline references are retained, and the Q05 baseline/pilot sequence is independently supported by Matthew 4.5 and its rationale. This is not a claim that every course anchor was freshly verified online.

Hypothetical organisations, numeric examples, policies, capabilities, and approvals are assessment premises, not real-user instructions or claims about every Claude subscription. The quiz tests no API development or enterprise architecture design. Skills and Code Execution appear in the guide's preparation advice (p. 6), and retained questions assess their use, validation, and escalation at Associate level. Account Preferences and Project instructions mean permitted configuration, not changing the provider's underlying system instructions.
