# PCAO-F Practice Quiz — CCAO-F-aligned revision

**60 questions · 50 single-answer · 10 multiple-response · Suggested time: 120 minutes**

Revised 11 September 2026 against the supplied *Claude Certified Associate – Foundations Exam Guide*, Version 1.0, effective July 2026 (CCAO-F). PCAO-F and CCAO-F are distinct certifications; this edition follows the requested Claude-specific revision of the PCAO-F practice set.

This practice assessment contains 50 original/adapted items and 10 questions imported from Matthew Purcell's *Claude Certified Associate – Foundations: Full Practice Question Set*. Import provenance appears in the separate source map. These are practice questions, not live certification exam items.

## Instructions

- Select exactly the number of answers stated. **Select ONE** items have four options; **Select TWO** items have five. Every multiple-response item requires exactly two answers.
- The product context is Claude, including where an imported question refers simply to a Project. Assume only the capabilities and approvals stated in each scenario; do not infer availability on a particular subscription plan.
- Treat each question independently. Use its stated facts and policies without inventing additional approvals, evidence, or legal rules.
- Choose the best answer or answer set for those circumstances. Some alternatives may be useful in a different situation.
- Award one point only when the entire selected set matches the answer key. There is no partial credit or negative marking. This is a practice convention, not the certification's scaled-scoring model.
- Keep the answer key, source map, and revision audit closed until you finish; they reveal answers and objectives.

The seven domain weights follow the supplied official guide and the [PCAO-F blueprint](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications/pcao-f#what-it-tests), rounded to whole questions. The 50/10 format mix follows the requested practice design. Practice scores do not convert into certification scaled scores or guarantee readiness. Model-family questions use the guide's Haiku/Sonnet/Opus distinctions, without testing specific model releases, prices, or interface locations.
